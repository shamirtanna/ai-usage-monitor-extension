Privacy Policy

AI Usage Monitor and Skill Protection · Last updated: 2026-10-03

This extension helps you keep your own thinking sharp while using AI assistants. It is built privacy-first: by default, nothing you type leaves your browser. This policy explains exactly what data is handled, where it goes, and the choices you control.

What the extension does

When you send a prompt to a supported AI site (currently Claude), the extension reads the text of that prompt, classifies it (e.g. a lookup, a draft, or a judgment task), and , for high-stakes prompts , may show you a brief nudge to bring your own thinking before you send. It also keeps simple statistics so you can review your patterns.

What is stored, and where

  • Your interaction history is stored locally in your browser (Chrome's extension storage) , on your device, not on our servers. The dashboard (popup) reads from this local store.
  • You can turn off "Store prompt text" at any time. With it off, the extension still classifies and shows statistics, but the actual wording of your prompts is not kept.
  • We do not store your prompt content on any server.

Smart classification modes (your choice)

Classifying ambiguous or high-stakes prompts more accurately can use an AI model. You choose how:

  • Local (private): a built-in rule-based classifier runs entirely in your browser. No prompt data leaves your device. No account or key required.
  • Your own key: the extension sends the prompt directly from your browser to Anthropic's API using your own API key. The prompt goes to your Anthropic account, governed by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. It does not pass through our servers.
  • Shared server (default): the prompt is sent to our hosted classifier, which calls Anthropic on your behalf using our key, subject to daily usage limits. In this mode your prompt passes through our server in transit to be classified; we do not store the prompt content. We keep only anonymous usage counts (see below). Because the prompt reaches our account and Anthropic in this mode, we recommend not using it for highly sensitive content; use Local or Your-own-key mode for that.

What our server logs (metadata only)

In Shared-server mode, our server keeps anonymous usage metadata only , a random per-installation identifier and counts of how many classification requests were made (to enforce fair daily usage limits). This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or account, and we do not log the text of your prompts.

Third parties

Depending on the mode you choose, prompt text may be sent to Anthropic (the maker of Claude) for classification , either via our server (Shared-server mode) or directly from your browser (Your-own-key mode). Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by Anthropic's own privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data with any other third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never use your prompts for advertising or profiling.
  • We never store your prompt content on our servers.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or account identity.

Your controls

  • Turn nudges on/off at any time.
  • Turn "Store prompt text" on/off at any time.
  • Choose your classification mode (Local / Your own key / Shared server).
  • Clear your local history by removing the extension's stored data (via Chrome's extension settings).
